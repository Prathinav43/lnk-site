LNK

In development

Your phone and your PC, on one private link.

Lnk pairs an Android phone with a Linux desktop over your own Tailscale network, so your photos and calls reach your PC without passing through anyone else's server.

What it does

  • Gallery on your desktop

    Browse the photos on your phone from your PC, by folder, with thumbnails. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  • Calls without reaching for the phone

    Incoming calls appear on your desktop. Answer or decline them there and keep working.

  • Pairing that remembers

    Sign in once on each device. Lnk finds your PC on its own after that, even when its address changes.

What it needs from you

Two things Lnk cannot do on your behalf. Both are worth knowing before you start.

Both devices on the same Tailscale network
Lnk carries your data over Tailscale rather than a server of its own, which is what keeps your photos private. Signing each device in to Tailscale is something you do in Tailscale itself — Lnk has no way to do it for you.
Battery optimisation turned off for Lnk on your phone
Android powers down the Wi-Fi radio soon after your screen turns off, which drops the connection. Setting Lnk to unrestricted in your battery settings keeps it alive. On Samsung, Xiaomi and OnePlus phones this is required rather than optional.

Where it is now

Working, in daily use, not yet packaged

The Android app and the Linux desktop app both run, and the account system behind them is live. What's left is packaging them into something you can install without a developer toolchain. Downloads will appear here when that's done.

Lnk sends email for two reasons only: to verify your address when you create an account, and to reset your password if you ask it to. There is no mailing list and nothing else is ever sent.
